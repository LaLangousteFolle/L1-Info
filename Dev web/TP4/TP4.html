It's Unbelievably Soft

The Blåhaj is made from a short-pile plush fabric that feels incredibly smooth to the touch. Whether you're hugging it after a long day or just letting it sit on your bed, its softness alone is worth every penny of its modest price tag.

The Size Is Just Right

At 100 cm long, the Blåhaj is big enough to hug like a pillow but not so massive that it takes over your entire room. It hits a perfect sweet spot between a decorative plushie and a full-on body pillow.

Want to know the exact dimensions?

The Blåhaj measures 100 cm in length, 37 cm in width, and 26 cm in height. It weighs around 700 grams, making it light enough to carry around the house without any effort. IKEA has kept these dimensions consistent across all releases, so every Blåhaj is the same reassuring size.

It's a Genuine Emotional Support Animal

Don't let the price fool you — the Blåhaj has helped countless people deal with stress, loneliness, and anxiety. There's something about its dopey smile and outstretched fins that makes it impossible not to feel just a little bit better when it's around.

Incredible Value for Money

At around €25 depending on your country, the Blåhaj is one of the best-value plushies on the market. You'd easily pay double or triple that for a comparable stuffed animal at any toy store, and none of them would have the same cultural cachet.

It's a Certified Internet Icon

The Blåhaj has amassed a massive following online, spawning countless memes, fan art pieces, and even dedicated social media accounts. Owning one immediately connects you to a warm, welcoming community of people who just really love this shark.

It Makes a Perfect Gift

Struggling to find a gift for someone? The Blåhaj works for virtually anyone — kids, adults, students, stressed-out friends, or that one person in your life who's impossible to shop for. It's thoughtful, fun, and universally adored.
