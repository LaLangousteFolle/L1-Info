The Chinese new years is nowadays one of the most celebrated event of the year, celebrated by more than a sixth of the modial population not only in China, but also in the US thanks to a massive imigration after the gold rush in the 1840's. It's the most important, social and economic event in China.

It stars every years at the second new moon after winter solstice, their calendar exist since the 14th century befor Christ which corresponds to a start between january 21st and february 19th as China adopted the gregorian calendar, which exists since the 16th century, in the 1912. Each years is named after 12 different animals, the chinese zodiac signs. The two most important are dragon and rabbit, dragon is told to be chineses descends from dragons, and rabbit comes from a myth saying that Shang E jumped on the moon with a rabbit. 

Each year, many people clean their house, buy new clothes, get new haircuts pay the debts as it is know to bring luck and good fortune is households. Later in the night they release rabbit shaped paper lanterns. They cook dumpling with their family and eat long noodles, and wear red as it is know to bring luck, and avoid black which is associated to death.
